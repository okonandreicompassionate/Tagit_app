Tagit

Scan someone once.
Remember them forever.

Meet someone at a party, scan their code, and they're saved with the night attached — who they were, where you met, one tap to add them on Snap.

Scan with your phone

Download the app

Tap the button on an Android phone, or scan the code from a computer screen. About 60 MB.

Installing it

  1. 1

    Tap Download the app. Your browser saves the file.

  2. 2

    Open it from your notifications or your Downloads folder.

  3. 3

    Android will warn you it's from an unknown source. That's normal for any app not from the Play Store — choose Install anyway.

  4. 4

    Open Tagit. It lands straight on the camera — that's the whole app.

iPhone isn't ready yet

Apple only allows app installs through the App Store, which needs a paid developer account. It's coming — for now iPhone users can run Tagit through the free Expo Go app. Ask and we'll send a link.

This is an early build

Things will be rough and some features are still being wired up. It won't post anything anywhere, and nothing is shared until someone scans your code.
