Tagit

Scan someone once.
Remember them forever.

Meet someone at a party, scan their code, and they're saved with the night attached — who they were, where you met, one tap to add them on Snap.

Scan with your phone

Download the app

Tap the button on an Android phone, or scan the code from a computer screen. About 60 MB.

What it does

Scan someone, add them on Snap Opens straight to the camera. One scan, one tap, and they're in your Snapchat — nobody spells a username. One scan links you both.
It remembers the night Everyone you meet is saved with where and when. Run into them again and it starts a streak.
Find something to go to A full-screen feed of concerts, parties and pop-ups. Host your own in a few taps — public, or invite-only for your friends.
Rank you can't buy Points come only from scanning the code at a venue and meeting real people. There is no way to earn them sitting at home.

Installing it

  1. 1

    Tap Download the app. Your browser saves the file.

  2. 2

    Open it from your notifications or your Downloads folder.

  3. 3

    Android will warn you it's from an unknown source. That's normal for any app not from the Play Store — choose Install anyway.

  4. 4

    Open Tagit and sign in with your phone number or email. That's so your card follows you to a new phone — sign in with the same one and everything comes back.

iPhone isn't ready yet

Apple only allows app installs through the App Store, which needs a paid developer account. It's coming — for now iPhone users can run Tagit through the free Expo Go app. Ask and we'll send a link.

Tap instead of scan

If your phone does NFC, you can tap a sticker or wristband to check in instead of aiming a camera in the dark. Two phones can't tap each other — that isn't possible on any phone — so people are still scanned with the camera.

This is an early build

Things will be rough and some features are still being wired up. It won't post anything anywhere, and nothing is shared until someone scans your code.
